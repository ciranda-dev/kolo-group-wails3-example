# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- The live room in `internal/room`: `Open`, `Create`, `KeyPackage`, `Add`, `Join`, `Remove`, `Send`, `Next`, `Members`, `Allow` and `ServeConn`. A room is an MLS group whose Commits the admin settles through kolo-braid and sends to the members on the room's gossip topic. Messages are sealed with a key exported from the MLS epoch, travel as signed gossip messages, and show in the causal order of the ids they name (`Display`).
- Admission: `Node.Allow` for the swarm lets in only the devices of a room's current epoch, and `ServeConn` refuses and closes the others. A removed device is disconnected, refused after a short window and reads nothing sent after its removal.
- Protective bounds in `room.Limits`, each configurable with a written basis in `docs/limits.md`: message size, rate, parents, hold, queues, window and tree size.
- `ServeConn` serves gossip on the unidirectional streams of a swarm connection and kolo-sync on its bidirectional ones.
- Tests: rooms of 3 and 64 devices on 127.0.0.1 over real swarm connections, the removal of a device, admission, hostile messages (wrong epoch, forged sender, replay, oversized body, flood), the order of messages with property tests, and fuzz tests of every decoder.

- Admin segments in `internal/room`: every admin device writes the messages of the room into its own kolo-log segments (one epoch, at most `SegmentEntries` entries each) and a control log (Commits, SettledCommits, the opening and closing of segments, the control logs of other admins), and pushes them to the pins with kolo-pin. Members follow those logs with kolo-sync, derive their entry keys and serve what they hold to the other members of the room.
- `settle.Pins` over the control logs (`roomPins`), with the new `List` (paged and ordered) and `Delete` of kolo-mls.
- Catch-up: a device that was away applies the settled Commits it missed epoch by epoch and reads the segments from the admins, from other members and from the pins; an entry it cannot verify or open is reported with `ErrCatchUp` and never skipped without a report.
- Stored receipts and republishing: a message sent while no admin is online is kept until a segment holds it, shows `Stored` when one does, and is published again meanwhile; `Status` reports receipts, followed logs, catching up and admins online.
- Persistence: rooms, MLS state, epoch keys, authors, receipts, logs and the admins' braid are reopened by `Open`; a removed device forgets its room.
- `Promote` makes a second admin through the admins' braid and a settled Commit that changes the roles.
- One connection for gossip, kolo-sync and the braid, with a tag byte on each stream.
- Protective bounds in `room.Limits` with their basis in `docs/limits.md`: `SegmentEntries`, `KeepSegments`, `MaxLogs`, `PinGap`, `PinTTL`, `PinWait`, `CatchUpWait`, `RepublishAfter` and `MaxReceipts`.
- Tests: segments, catch-up, restart and pins at 3 and 4 devices, a run of 16 and one of 64 devices with 2 admins, 2 pins, an admin killed in the middle of an epoch and 10,000 messages over three epochs, hostile segments (a bad signature, a key that does not open, an unknown author, a gap, a rewritten history, a bad SettledCommit), the decoders and their fuzz tests.

- History for new members in `internal/room`: an admin writes, for each epoch it enters, a history link (the key of the epoch before, sealed under the key of the epoch) to its control log, and `Room.History` reads the messages of the epochs before the join, backward, through the chain of links and the admins' segments (from the admins, the members or the pins). A message that cannot be read is reported with `ErrCatchUp` and its cause, a chain that cannot go on with `ErrHistory`, a refused link with `ErrLink`.
- The history rule (`AllHistory`, the default, `SinceEpoch` or `NoHistory`) is chosen in `Create` and recorded in a GroupContext extension (0xF003) that the settled Commits sign and the Welcomes carry. No link is written for an epoch the rule leaves out, a link written after a segment of its epoch is refused, and a rule is never retroactive.
- Tests: a joiner of epoch 40 reads epochs 1 to 39, `SinceEpoch`, `NoHistory`, a member removed at epoch 20, late, out-of-policy, forged and missing links, undecryptable entries, a message of a device that left before the join, a restart, the pins, and fuzz tests of the new decoders. `docs/security.md` states the cost in forward secrecy and `docs/limits.md` the measured cost of the links.

- Posters in `internal/room`: the room policy (the GroupContext extension 0xF003, signed by every settled Commit) says who may post: every member (the default), `Policy.Posters` (persons), `Policy.PosterDevices`, `Policy.AdminsPost`, or a union. A post by a device that may not post is refused with `ErrNotPoster` by `Send`, by the validator of every node (verdict Reject) and by an admin's segment writer, and reported by a reader of a segment. A member that may not post is a read-only member: it reads what its history rule allows, live and before its join. A message is judged by the policy of its own epoch.
- `SetPolicy` and `Room.Policy`: an admin changes the posters, the history rule and the read link with one settled Commit, for the epochs from that Commit on and never before. kolo-mls accepts the change of the extension, so no library change was needed. A Commit that does not fit one gossip message is refused with `ErrTooLarge` before it is made, for `SetPolicy` and for `Promote`.
- The read link: `Room.Link` and `Node.JoinLink`. A room with a read link (it needs a list of posters or `AdminsPost`) gives a link; a device that joins with it reads the room live and its history through the same chain of links as a member, is not a leaf of the tree and cannot post. Each admin writes the state of every epoch (the key, the roles, the policy and the certificates of the admins and posters) to its control log, sealed under a key derived from a read secret, in a new control record `ctlRead`. Turning the read link off and on again with `SetPolicy` makes a new link and stops the readers of the old one at the next epoch. A node that has a room with a read link, or reads one, admits any device. A reader room is not kept across a restart. `docs/security.md` says that a public read link gives no secrecy against anyone who holds it.
- `Room.Leave`: a member asks the admins to remove its person (one admin answers, so two admins make one Commit), waits until they have or until its context ends, and stops the room on the device. A reader's `Leave` only stops and forgets its room.
- Tests: a non-poster refused at the validators and at an admin, a read-only member that reads live and history, `SetPolicy` holding from the next epoch and not before (also across a restart), a history rule changed by a Commit that is never retroactive, a policy that does not fit, a reader that joins by a link, reads across epochs and history and cannot post, a new link that stops the old reader, a bad link, `Leave` with one admin, with two and with none, the removed device that learns it was removed, the segment that is pushed while it is forgotten, and fuzz tests of the new decoders (the policy, the state, the link, the opened state and the new control record). `docs/limits.md` has the measured sizes.

- Commits by reference in `internal/room`: a settled Commit is announced on the room's topic by its SettledCommit alone (a quorum of settler signatures, from an admin, for the current epoch; `ErrCommitRef` otherwise) and pulled by hash, as pieces of at most 1 MiB, from the control logs of the admins through kolo-sync, from any device that holds them. A piece set that is cut short, or that hashes to another name, is passed over for another admin's. There is no size threshold and no inline mode, so a Commit of any size, and a room of 3, use the same path.
- The encrypted tree served by admins and pins (`internal/room`): the admin that adds writes the sealed tree into a log of its own, in pieces under its hash, names it in its control log (`ctlTree`) and pushes it to the pins; the other admins, and the members that set `Config.ServeTree` (off by default), replicate it and serve it to joiners. `Join` pulls the tree from the admins the invite names, then from the pins and any device that answers, and checks its hash and the Welcome. The tree log of a Welcome goes once the Welcome is used (kolo-mls `ReleaseWelcome`, when its device first sends) and a later epoch has come; an unused one stays.
- `ErrCommitRef` and `ErrTree`.
- `Policy.SinceCommit`: `SetPolicy` with `SinceEpoch` and `SinceCommit` starts the rule at the epoch of its own Commit, so a caller that wants "from now on" needs no epoch, and a Commit that settles first does not move the start one epoch late.
- `Room.Reader` reports a room joined with a read link, `Status.Peers` counts the other devices of the room's current epoch connected now, and `Room.MembersPage` returns a page of `Members` with the number of devices, so a long member list is not copied whole for each page.
- Tests: pieces of any size, pieces that do not hash to their key, a Commit cut short and read from another admin, a Commit of a 30,000-leaf group (2,461,142 bytes, built in one process) reaching 64 members in 1.04 s, a joiner that gets its tree from members that serve with the admin away, from the pins with the admin away and members that do not serve, from the admin with the pins down, from a pin with no device up, a member that does not serve holding and sending no tree, a joiner with no server waiting until its context ends and joining when the admin returns, a joiner refused a wrong tree, a tree log that goes with its used Welcome while an unused one stays, 100 joiners of a 30,000-leaf group pulling its 14.5 MB tree from the admin and from two pins, a forged announcement refused, and fuzz tests of the new decoders. `KOLO_GROUP_LEAVES`, `KOLO_GROUP_JOINERS` and `KOLO_GROUP_FETCHERS` set the size of the large tests.

- The window: create a room (name, history rule, who may post, read link), the list of rooms, the conversation with replies, sending and the order of `Next`, the messages kept across a restart, scrolling back (the messages this device was told of, then the history before the join), members a page at a time with promoting (a device that is offline gets the offer when it is back), removing and choosing who may post, adding devices by key package and welcome, the read link, the status of a room (epoch, catching up, pins, the devices of the room connected), room settings (the policy) and leaving, and the settings of the app (network, pins, relays, keys). The frontend is Vite and React in English, Spanish and Portuguese, in the style of the other Kolo apps.
- `-headless`: an always-on device with no window over the same data folder, with a JSON config file (`-config`) and the flags and variables of the other apps (`-bootstrap`, `-listen`, `-pin`, `-pin-file`, `-pin-ttl`, `-lookup-interval`, `-relay`, `-relay-file`). It prints a key package when it has no room, joins the welcome in `-welcome-file`, and, once an admin promotes it, writes the segments of its rooms and pushes them to the pins.
- `task test-30k` (`scripts/test-30k.sh`): the gossip simulator at 30,000 nodes, `TestMeasureReaders` and the room tests of a 30,000-leaf group, in one documented target that no workflow runs.
- Tests: the window's service over real devices on 127.0.0.1 (talking and replying, what the window keeps across a restart, posters and policy changes, history scrolled back, promoting, removing, leaving, the read link and its reader, status, one action at a time and its cancel), a headless device that joins from a file, becomes an admin, pushes to a pin and serves a member that was away, one data folder run by the window and by `-headless`, the config file and its order, the README's quick start run for real, and `npm test` with a window test in headless Chrome that includes a room of 30,000 devices read a page at a time.

### Changed

- `Node.Rooms` no longer lists a room that this device left or was removed from, and `Node.Room` returns `ErrRemoved` for it.
- `Promote` sends its offer again every `RepublishAfter` until the device answers, so a device that is offline or not in the mesh yet when it starts is promoted once it is; it waited until its context ended before.
- **Breaking**: only the admins and the pins hold and serve the tree logs of joiners. A member that is no admin no longer replicates the tree log of an Add (14.5 MB per Add and per member at 30,000 leaves, hundreds of gigabytes for the room) and sends no byte of it, unless its node sets the new `Config.ServeTree` (off by default). `Join` asks the admins that the invite names first, then the pins (in a random order) and any other device after `PinWait`, and with no server it asks again, each time after twice the wait before up to `PinWait`, until its context ends. A joiner needs an admin, a pin or a serving member reachable. The tests use `github.com/prometheus/client_golang` directly to count what a pin serves, so `go.mod` lists it as a direct requirement.
- **Breaking**: the message kind 2 of the room's topic carries only a SettledCommit and no Commit, the invite carries the header, length and hash of a tree log and not the tree, and `Join` needs the devices of the room (or a pin) to be reachable to fetch it; a build of the earlier format does not read either. A Commit is no longer refused with `ErrTooLarge` when it does not fit one gossip message (`SetPolicy`, `Promote`, `Add`): it is carried by reference. `ErrTooLarge` stays for messages.
- `Join` admits any device while it pulls the tree (see `docs/security.md`). A device that the last Commit removed may still read the room's logs for `PastEpoch`, so that it pulls the Commit that removes it.
- Fixed: `Room.commit` read `Room.admin` before it took `Room.mu`, which a promotion writes under; the catch-up tests read the MLS member without the lock the room's goroutines hold. Both could be reported by `-race`.
- **Breaking**: `GroupService.Rooms` returns the rooms and an error, `Start` must be called first, and `-headless` no longer returns an error. The data folder has a `layout.json` ({"layout":1}), a person key and a device key, a certificate and four stores; a folder of another layout is refused.
- `go.mod` requires kolo-relay directly (the window reads the state of the connection relays).
- `Create` accepts the whole `Policy`; `Policy` gains `PosterDevices` and `AdminsPost`. The policy extension of an earlier build (history rule only) does not decode: create the room again. The history rule under `AllHistory` now carries the epoch the chain starts at, which `SetPolicy` sets, and a device reads its past by the rule it joined under. A link of an epoch before the join that the rule of the join leaves out is dropped without a `ErrLink` report. A key package lists the policy extension among its capabilities.
- The members close their connections to a removed device after `PastEpoch` instead of at once, and refuse a new connection from it at once.
- A message sealed in an epoch before the join never shows in `Next`, even when an admin stored it in a later segment: it belongs to `History`.

- A sealed message carries the signature of its author, and the id of a message is a hash of what the author signed, the same for every holder of a copy (it was gossip's id). The id of a message sent by an older build differs.
- A device publishes at most four fifths of `Rate`, waiting for the rest, and a message whose time is older than its envelope's is accepted (`MaxSkew` bounds only a time ahead).
- The validator accepts a message of an older epoch from a current member when the node holds that epoch's key.
- `room.Config` requires `Store` (the logs) and takes `Pins`; `Open` reopens the rooms of its backend.
- The kolo-mls interface `settle.Pins` is the paged one (`List(ctx, prefix, after, max)` and `Delete`); `go.mod` requires kolo-secure and kolo-transport directly.

- `room.Config` has a `Backend` (the store of the admins' braid and of the Commits of the rooms this device creates) and `BraidLimits`. `Join` takes the welcome that `Add` returns.
- The docs say what is built and what is planned: `Promote` to Settler, Commits by reference, the window and `-headless` are not built.
- `go.mod` requires kolo-braid, kolo-codec, kolo-log, kolo-sync and golang.org/x/crypto directly.

### Fixed

- A device that an admin removes now learns that it was removed. The other members closed their connections to it as soon as they applied the Commit, often before the Commit reached it, so it never set its removed state (in a room that had passed 8 epochs this happened in 5 of 10 runs; with the delay in none of 15, `TestRemovedDeviceLearnsItWasRemoved`). The Commit itself is the signed notice; no other message is needed.
- An admin with few kept segments (`KeepSegments`) no longer forgets a segment while it is pushed to the pins. The push took its logs out of the list of logs to push and the trim of old segments then closed one of them; the push failed and was tried again for ever, and `Status().PinError` stayed at "pin: no pinning peer accepted the log" (5 of 15 runs with `KeepSegments: 1` and two pins; `TestSegmentBeingPushedIsNotForgotten` fails on the old code every time).

### Security

- A post is judged by the policy of its epoch and by the current policy (or, for a stored one, by the policy of the segment that holds it), and only from a device that was a leaf of its epoch, so a member that may not post can no longer post by sealing under the key of an epoch before the restriction. A message of the previous epoch in flight is still judged by its own epoch while the window lasts; after a restart that window is gone. Each device records the first epoch it saw every author in (a new record kind).
- `SetPolicy`, `Promote` and `Remove` build their Commit from the view of the moment they commit, under the lock that applies the other admins' Commits. A `Promote` that waited for its device committed the policy it read before the wait and undid a `SetPolicy` settled meanwhile (a posters restriction, a new read secret or a history cut); `Remove` finds the leaves of the person in that view instead of decoding the whole tree.
- A member checks the settler signatures, group, epoch and braid of a SettledCommit that a control log holds before it fetches the Commit it names, and passes over one it has reported, so a SettledCommit that no quorum signed no longer makes every member reassemble and hash its Commit (up to `MaxCommitBytes`) on every turn of the sync loop. It is reported once with `ErrCommitRef`.
- A second Commit settled for one epoch (an announcement, a candidate of the control logs or a late SettledCommit of the epoch before, each with a quorum of signatures) is reported once with the new `ErrEquivocation`, in a `CatchingUp` event. With one settler, the creator, the room trusts that device not to fork it.
- An entry of a segment sealed under an epoch after the segment's own is reported with `ErrWrongEpoch` and the entries after it are read; only an entry of the segment's epoch or before waits for the room to catch up. A hostile admin's entry of a far epoch stopped the reading of its segment with no report and kept `Status().CatchingUp` true and the device on the pins.
- `History` keeps every key that the history links of an epoch give (the one most links agree on first, the others in a new record kind) and opens each message with each of them, and reports a disagreement with `ErrLink` in its page. One admin's link that opened to a wrong key was taken, persisted and ended `History` for every later joiner.
- A tree log is served only to the admins, to a device whose Welcome into its epoch the serving admin made, to a member that joined in its epoch and, while its epoch is the current one, to the members (a member that sets `Config.ServeTree` replicates it then). A reader of the read link, a removed device and a member after the epoch are refused; before, every member and, in a room with a read link, any device could pull it (14.5 MB at 30,000 leaves) again and again. The per-peer byte budget of kolo-sync's server bounds what one peer pulls over time.
- `followPin` reads whether a replica still lacks entries under the room's log lock; it read the closed flag and size that the sync loop writes without it, a data race.
- An admin reports a Welcome it could not release after it lets go of the room's lock: with a full event queue the report held the lock and stopped the admin's Commits until `Next` was read.
- The validator takes the sender's rate token before it checks the author signature of a message and the settler signatures of an announcement, so a device that floods is refused before it costs signature checks.
- An admin starts one removal at a time for a person that asks to leave and drops the requests sent again meanwhile; each request started a goroutine that decoded the whole tree under the room's lock and waited for the commit lock.
- A segment whose entry keys failed once still reports a gap: one flag served both reports. **Breaking** (wire): the room secret is exported under its own label `kolo-group/room-secret/v0` instead of the topic's, and the message key of an epoch under `kolo-group/epoch-key/v0` instead of `kolo-group/history/v0`; a room made by an earlier build does not open.
- An admin that opens a room writes the history link and the readers' state of its current epoch when its control log lacks them: a crash between applying a Commit and writing them lost them, and with one admin the chain of links ended there for every later joiner.
- `Open`, `Create`, `Join` and `JoinLink` try the swarm join of a room's topic again while no server of the discovery network answers (`kolodht.ErrNoNodes`), each time after twice the wait before up to `PinWait`, until their context ends: a device that restarted in a moment the network did not answer failed to open every room. This was the failure of `TestCatchUpWithSixtyFourNodes` under `-race` ("kologossip: swarm join: kolodht: no server answered" at the restart of an admin); no data race was reported in those runs.
- **Breaking** (wire): an admin starts a new control log at each epoch it enters. The old log ends with a `ctlNext` record that names the new one and the new one starts with a `ctlPrev` record that names the old one (two new control record kinds). An invite and a read link name only the current logs, so a joiner no longer pulls and indexes every Commit the room ever had (in the test, 6 epochs later, the joiner held a third or less of the entries of the admin's control logs, the old code all of them); `History` follows the earlier logs back, one epoch at a time, only when it reads that far. A member forgets the control logs of the epochs it has left past `KeepSegments`, as it does with segments; an admin keeps its own, since a member that is behind pulls the Commits from it.
- A room's gossip topic admits only the devices of its current epoch, and of the previous one while its window lasts (kolo-gossip `TopicOptions.AllowPeer`, asked again with `RefreshPeers` after each epoch and once its window has ended); a room with a read link, or read with one, admits any device. A node that admits any device to its connections, for a room with a read link or while a `Join` pulls its tree, no longer tells such a device the topics of its closed rooms nor lets it into their meshes: a holder of one room's read link received the envelopes (author keys, timing, sizes) of the node's other rooms. This needs the kolo-gossip branch with `AllowPeer` (its `Publish` returns the message, which `publish` now reads the id from).
- `MaxLogs` counts only the replicas a room follows. An admin's own control logs, one for each epoch, counted against it and were never released, so a second admin stopped following the other admins' control logs after about `MaxLogs` epochs (at `MaxLogs` 20 the old code stopped at epoch 11). `History` follows the control logs of the epoch it loads and of the one after, not the whole chain at once, and forgets each once read: a joiner that read the history of about `MaxLogs` epochs filled `MaxLogs` (`ErrTooManyLogs`) and could no longer follow the room. A device keeps the records that name earlier control logs only for the epochs before its join, once each, and lets them go once read; a member kept one for every admin and every epoch.
- A `ctlPrev` record counts only as the first record of a control log, with that log's epoch, and a `ctlNext` only when it goes forward; one forged `ctlPrev` made every joiner's `History` wait about twice `CatchUpWait` for each epoch (21 s for 4 epochs at a `CatchUpWait` of 3 s in the test; now the whole test takes 1.3 s). A control log that no peer serves is waited for once by `History`, reported in its page and not waited for again, nor the logs it named (4 epochs took 14 s at a `CatchUpWait` of 2 s, under 4 s now).
- `ctlNext` and `ctlPrev` are applied only from the control log of an admin, as `ctlOpen` and `ctlDir` are.
- A record of a control log that names an epoch past the one after its log's own is reported with `ErrWrongEpoch` and passed over, since an admin writes to its log of an epoch only while the room is in it. A `ctlOpen` of a far epoch made a member follow a segment that it waited for for ever, kept `Status().CatchingUp` true and the device on the pins, and was never trimmed.
- A member keeps `KeepSegments` closed segments and, counted apart, as many control logs it has left: the two shared one count, so a member kept about half the segments it was set for (2 of 4 in the test).
- `Open` reopens the rooms together: each waited for the topic join of the one before, so a node with many rooms waited for the discovery network once for each (4 rooms whose joins take 1 s each: 4.0 s before, about 1 s now).
- `Limits.PeerBytesPerSecond` and `Limits.PeerBurstBytes` set the byte budget for each peer of the node's kolo-sync server. Before, the room used kolo-sync's defaults and could not change them, so any member could make an admin upload up to 32 MiB a second to it for the whole epoch; the defaults are still kolo-sync's (32 MiB a second, 256 MiB), chosen and not measured.
- Docs: `docs/security.md`, `docs/how-it-works.md` and `docs/limits.md` say what the code does after the fixes of the first security review: posters judged by two policies, the window of the previous epoch, links that give several keys, stored entries of a later epoch, the signature check before a Commit is fetched, the tree log serving rule and the per-peer budget, the topics a device admitted for one room sees, every member holding the read secret, what sizes tell a pin, the single settler, and the growth of the admins' own control logs.
